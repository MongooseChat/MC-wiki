Traits
=======

Positive Traits
---------------

This is a list of the vanilla positive traits and a description of what they do:

.. list-table::
    :widths: 15 5 50 50
    :header-rows: 1

    * - Name
      - Points
      - Description
      - Effect

    * - Adrenaline Junkie
      - -4
      - Moves faster when highly panicked.
      - Adds a flat bonus of 0.20 or 0.25 to the character's base speed at Strong or Extreme Panic, which increases walking, running, and sprinting speed.
        
    * - Angler
      - -4
      - +1 Fishing
      - | +1 Fishing 
        | Knows how to make and fix a fishing rod and how to make chum. 
        | Improves foraging.
    
    * - Artisan
      - -2
      - Better at pottery and glass crafts.
      - | +1 Glassmaking 
        | +1 Pottery 
        | Unlocks several glass recipes.
        
    * - Athletic
      - -10
      - | Faster running speed. 
        | Can run for longer without tiring.
      - | +4 Fitness 
        | +20% running/sprinting speed. 
        | -20% running/sprinting endurance loss from the trait itself.
        
    * - Baseball Player
      - -4
      - +1 Long Blunt
      - | +1 Long Blunt 
        | Knows how to carve baseball bats.
        
    * - Blacksmith Knowledge
      - -6
      - Can use an anvil to create metal items.
      - | +1 Maintenance 
        | +2 Blacksmithing 
        | Unlocks several recipes.
        
    * - Brave
      - -4
      - Less prone to becoming panicked.
      - 30% panic, except for night terrors and phobias.

      - | +1 Axe 
        | +1 Long Blunt

        | +1 Long Blunt

      - | +20% better vision at night 
        | Improves foraging.
        
    * - Crafty
      - -3
      - Increased XP gains for Crafting skills.
      - 130% XP for all crafting skills
        
    * - Dextrous
      - -2
      - | Transfers inventory items quickly. 
        | Faster rope climbing speed, and less chance of falling. 
        | Faster weapon shouldering time.
      - 50% inventory transfer time.
        
    * - Eagle Eyed
      - -4
      - | Faster visibility fade. 
        | Higher visibility arc. 
        | Weapon sights more effective at long range.
      - | Character has a wider field of view. 
        | Improves foraging.
        
    * - Fast Healer
      - -6
      - Recovers quickly from injuries and illness.
      - | Does not apply to exercise fatigue. 
        | Recently inflicted injuries have less severity. 
        | Including Scratches, Lacerations, Lodged Bullets, Deep wounds (with/without glass), Bites and Fractures. (Check Health for more details.)
        
    * - Fast Learner
      - -6
      - Increases XP gains.
      - 130% XP for all skills except Strength and Fitness.
        
    * - Fast Reader
      - -2
      - Takes less time to read books.
      - 130% reading speed.

      - -2
      - +1 First Aid
      - +1 First Aid

      - +2 Fitness.

      - -2
      - +1 Agriculture
      - | +1 Agriculture 
        |Improves foraging. 
        | Unlocks several recipes.

      - | +1 Aiming

    * - Inconspicuous
      - -4
      - Less likely to be spotted by zombies.
      - 50% chance of zombies spotting you.

      - | +2 Cooking

      - Larger perception radius.
      - | 200% perception radius.

      - -2
      -  Needs to eat less regularly.
      -  75% hunger.
        
    * - Low Thirst

      - Needs less water to survive.

      - | +2 Masonry 
        | Unlocks several recipes.
        
    * - Nutritionist
      - -2
      - Can see the nutritional values of any food.
      - | Allows the player to see the nutritional values of any food, even those that aren't packaged. 
        | Improves foraging.
        
    * - Organized
      - -4
      - Increased container inventory capacity.
      - 130% capacity for all containers, including boxes, cupboards and cars.
        
    * - Outdoorsy
      - -2
      - Less affected by harsh weather conditions.
      - | 10% chance of catching a cold. 1% or 1.25% chance of getting scratched/lacerated while walking or running through trees. 
        | Improves foraging.
        
    * - Resilient
      - -4
      - | Less prone to disease. 
        | Slower rate of zombification.

    * - Sewer

      - | +1 Tailoring

    * - Tinkerer

      - +1 Maintenance
      - +1 Maintenance

      - | +1 Mechanics

    * - Wakeful

    * - Whittler

      - Can carve wood and bone items.

        | Can craft Bone Fishing Hook, Bone Sewing Needle, and sharpen animal bones. 
        | Improves foraging.
